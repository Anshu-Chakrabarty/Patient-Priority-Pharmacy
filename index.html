Frequently Asked

Workers Often Ask Us…

Does workers' comp pay for my prescriptions?
+
Yes. Workers' compensation is designed to cover all medically necessary prescription medications related to your workplace injury. Patient Priority Pharmacy bills your workers' comp carrier directly so you pay nothing out of pocket.
What if my claim is disputed or my insurer isn't paying?
+
We can work on a lien basis — meaning you still receive your medications now, and payment is resolved when your case settles. We're experienced in navigating disputed claims and will advocate for you.
How fast will I receive my medications?
+
Once enrolled, most patients receive their first shipment within 1–3 business days. We work quickly to get your medications to you so your recovery isn't delayed.
Can I still use Patient Priority if I was injured in an auto accident?
+
Absolutely. We also serve personal injury (PI) and auto injury (no-fault) cases. If you were injured in any accident that involves an insurance claim, we can help.
