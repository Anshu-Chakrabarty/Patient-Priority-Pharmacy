Frequently Asked

Workers Often Ask Us…

Does workers' comp pay for my prescriptions?
+
Yes. Workers' compensation is designed to cover all medically necessary prescription medications related to your workplace injury. Patient Priority Pharmacy bills your workers' comp carrier directly so you pay nothing out of pocket.
What if my claim is disputed or my insurer isn't paying?
+
We can work on a lien basis — meaning you still receive your medications now, and payment is resolved when your case settles. We're experienced in navigating disputed claims and will advocate for you.
How fast will I receive my medications?
+
Once enrolled, most patients receive their first shipment within 1–3 business days. We work quickly to get your medications to you so your recovery isn't delayed.
Can I still use Patient Priority if I was injured in an auto accident?
+
Absolutely. We also serve personal injury (PI) and auto injury (no-fault) cases. If you were injured in any accident that involves an insurance claim, we can help.

Our Story

A Pharmacy Built for One Purpose

Patient Priority Pharmacy was founded with a singular focus: to be the most trusted workers' compensation and personal injury pharmacy in America.

Our Mission

We Exist to Remove Every Barrier Between Injured Workers and Their Medications

Patient Priority Pharmacy was born from a clear, frustrating reality: retail pharmacies weren't built for workers' compensation cases. They don't understand the billing rules. They can't fight insurance denials. They aren't equipped to handle litigated claims. And injured workers — already burdened with injury, lost wages, and legal complexity — were left to figure it out themselves.

We changed that. Patient Priority Pharmacy focuses exclusively on workers' compensation and personal injury cases. Our entire team — from enrollment specialists to claims coordinators to licensed pharmacists — is trained specifically in the state-specific nuances of WC and PI claims. We don't accept "denied" as a final answer. We appeal, advocate, and fight until your client or patient has the medications they were prescribed.

Based in Brownsburg, Indiana, we serve patients across all 50 states. We partner with plaintiff attorneys, healthcare providers, nurse case managers, adjusters, and injured workers — serving as an extension of your team, not just a vendor. Our promise is simple: if a physician prescribes it, we will deliver it.
